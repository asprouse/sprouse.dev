## About Vercel

Vercel is the agentic infrastructure company. We free people and agents to ship what's next.

The company has shaped web development for over a decade as creators of Next.js, v0, and AI SDK. Vercel serves companies including OpenAI, PayPal, Ramp, Supreme, and millions of developers worldwide.

## Role Summary

This position involves developing the AI Gateway platform, which provides a unified API for accessing hundreds of AI models from multiple providers. The engineer will focus on reliable, low-latency systems that handle rate limiting, intelligent failovers, and seamless integrations.

## Key Responsibilities

- Design and maintain the AI Gateway platform with emphasis on unified API endpoints, rate limit management, and intelligent failover mechanisms
- Write clean, efficient code with thorough testing for low-latency responses and stability for high-volume AI inference requests
- Collaborate across teams on integrating new AI providers and addressing scalability challenges
- Engage with open-source community and contribute to AI SDK and related projects
- Gather user feedback to drive innovations in enhanced billing unification, provider-agnostic authentication, and analytics

## Requirements

- Minimum 5+ years of relevant experience
- Strong JavaScript/TypeScript proficiency with backend development and cloud infrastructure knowledge
- Experience with AI/ML integrations, API gateways, distributed systems, or handling high-throughput services
- Open source project participation background
- Excellent collaborative communication skills
