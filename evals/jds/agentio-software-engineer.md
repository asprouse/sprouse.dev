## About Agentio

Agentio enables marketers to buy creator-led ads as easily and scalably as Meta or Google ads — automating sourcing, pricing, contracting, delivery, and measurement — so work that took months now happens in minutes. We're on a mission to transform a $800B digital ad ecosystem by shifting digital ad spend into the hands of creators.

Agentio has raised $56M to date at a $340M valuation, including a recent Series B led by Forerunner, which followed our Series A within a calendar year. Our investors include Benchmark, Craft, and AlleyCorp. Named to Forbes' Next Billion Dollar Startups, 2025.

## About the Role

We're looking for a senior software engineer with experience in early-stage or high-growth environments to join our team. As a foundational member of engineering, you'll work directly with our founders to design, build, and launch new product features — from first lines of code to production. Every week, you'll ship real functionality and have the autonomy to tackle meaningful, complex engineering problems.

You'll be tackling complex challenges in marketplace design, multimodal content understanding with large models, and campaign optimization in a high-performance environment where technical decisions directly shape product direction and business outcomes.
