## About You

You have a passion for doing things The Right Way, you believe Less is More, you want to focus on the foundations of system design and architecture (pacelc, consistency guarantees), you care as much about the code you do not write, you're tired of the hype treadmill and care about the fundamentals.

Your depth is on the backend and/or infrastructure. You may be the person building APIs, integrations, data pipelines, and distributed systems — or you may be the person scaling and hardening the cloud infrastructure underneath them. Either way, you've owned system design decisions, shipped them to production, and operated them at scale. You've been the person other engineers come to when something is broken or when a design needs a second opinion. You've led projects, mentored junior engineers, and made architectural decisions that stuck.

## About the Role

Members of Technical Staff at Anterior own problems end-to-end — from system design through to production. You'll drive technical direction, shape architecture, and set the bar for engineering quality.

A day's work may include:

- Scaling and hardening our AWS infrastructure to meet growing enterprise demand
- Analyzing request logs, debugging event streams, and improving system reliability
- Designing and building backend services, APIs, and data pipelines that power our AI platform
- Working directly with customers to understand real-world healthcare workflows, scope integrations, and translate requirements into product specs
- Building internal tooling to automate workflows and increase team leverage
- Writing evals to identify weaknesses and drive improvements in our LLM-powered systems
- Working across the stack when needed — from backend services to frontend — to ship complete solutions
- Contributing to Anterior's SDKs, developer documentation, and platform architecture
