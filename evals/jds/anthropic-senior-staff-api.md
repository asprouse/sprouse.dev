## About Anthropic

Anthropic's mission centers on creating reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems with focus on safety and societal benefit. The organization describes itself as a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders.

## Role Summary

This senior individual contributor position guides technical strategy across API Engineering, spanning five sub-teams: API Core, Capabilities, Knowledge, Distributability, and Agents. The role requires operating at the intersection of technical strategy and execution while partnering across Research, Inference, Platform, Infrastructure, and Safeguards divisions.

## Key Responsibilities

- Define and drive multi-year technical strategy across all API Engineering teams
- Lead highest-complexity, highest-impact initiatives spanning multiple teams
- Serve as primary technical decision-maker for organization-wide architectural choices
- Partner with Research on frontier capabilities integration
- Mentor Staff-level engineers across the organization
- Drive cross-functional alignment while identifying systemic technical risks
