## About Vercel

Vercel is the agentic infrastructure company. We free people and agents to ship what's next.

The company shapes web development as the team behind Next.js, v0, and AI SDK. Vercel serves major clients including OpenAI, PayPal, Ramp, and Supreme, with millions of developers using their products globally.

## About the Role

As an AI SDK Team member, you'll develop and enhance a TypeScript toolkit for building AI-native products and agents. This role involves collaborating with distributed engineers to create an SDK enabling developers worldwide to build AI-powered applications.

**Office Requirements:** Anchor days Monday, Tuesday, and Friday if within commuting distance of SF, NY, London, or Berlin offices; otherwise fully remote.

## What You Will Do

- Contribute to AI SDK design, implementation, and maintenance with high-performance standards
- Write clean, efficient, documented code with comprehensive testing
- Collaborate with product managers, designers, and infrastructure experts
- Engage with the open-source community and related projects
- Actively gather feedback from developers and users, and use it to drive continuous improvement

## About You

- Minimum 5+ years relevant experience
- Strong JavaScript/TypeScript proficiency and modern frontend tools experience
- Experience contributing to or participating in open source projects
- Excellent communication and collaborative abilities
