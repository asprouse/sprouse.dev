## About the Job

As a Software Engineering Manager in BigQuery's Analytics AI organization, you will build intelligent systems and experiences for next-generation data processing. You will develop agents, skills, and tools across the data lifecycle, creating AI-accelerated developer experiences that unify code and data development.

## Responsibilities

- Design and develop next-generation agentic solutions and data developer experiences such as data ingestion, data pipelines, data science, feature extraction, model training, and more
- Develop and maintain the technical roadmap of the team's charter
- Collaborate cross-functionally with Product, Data Scientists, Applied Scientists, and UX Designers to define roadmap for AI-native data journeys
- Iterate and enhance agentic frameworks by contributing to tool-use, memory, context retrieval, multi-step orchestration, and evaluation
- Lead the team on defining and executing on goal and long-term strategy
