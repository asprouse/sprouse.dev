Staff Software Engineer
New York, New York, USA

Datadog's Staff Engineers are technical leaders operating at the forefront of technology, building solutions that support the next five years of growth. The role combines individual contribution with technical leadership across the organization.

## What You'll Do

- Be the technical owner of multiple pieces of critical architecture in your area of the business
- Own delivery of the systems you architect from beginning-to-end, doing what it takes to get things shipped and at full scale in production
- Dive deep into performance of systems; inventing new approaches that bring efficiency at scale
- Participate in the staff engineering community to improve company-wide standards around software and systems engineering
- Lead design and delivery of industry-leading systems in areas such as data visualization, virtual runtime profiling, planet scale streaming, or new products

## Who You Are

- BS/MS/PhD in a scientific field or equivalent experience
- 10+ years of relevant engineering experience
- 4+ years of technical leadership experience, either as a manager or technical leader
- Led the design and delivery of industry-leading systems, either in their unique depth of technical expertise needed and/or in their scope of impact across a company's engineering org
- Demonstrated ability to use AI coding tools in day-to-day workflows and validate, critique, and refine AI-generated output
- Bonus: motivated to push the boundaries of how AI can improve software engineering best practices and contribute to building AI-enabled products

## Location & Compensation

- Location: New York, New York, USA
- Work Model: Hybrid
- Compensation: $234,000 — $300,000 USD
